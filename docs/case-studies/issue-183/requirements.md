## Scope and claim semantics

The request to support **all possible formal systems** is implemented as a
syntax-independent linked-program protocol: users may supply new finite
rewrite and inference systems, foundations, assumptions, and relative
semantics without adding a theory-specific runtime branch. No finite test can
prove correctness of every possible future theory or enumerate the infinite
deductive closure of a nontrivial theory. The claim is therefore tied to the
**finite executable acceptance scope** above: generic execution, explicit
bounds, mirrored runtimes, negative mutation tests, and the complete pinned
upstream corpus.

part of the RML trust path. The pinned builds establish source parity and
regression evidence; **Lean/Rocq cannot authorize RML execution**. The RML
acceptance cases must pass through its linked evaluator and exact witness
checks even when both native builds succeed.
